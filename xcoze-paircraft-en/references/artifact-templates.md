# Collaboration templates

Use only the fields you need for this round. Reuse existing project formats where possible.

## Brief for this round

- **User and situation** — Who faces what problem, and when?
- **Expected outcome** — What needs a decision or a deliverable?
- **Scope and things to preserve** — What changes, and what continues to work as before?
- **Open decisions** — What would each decision affect, and who makes it?
- **Success criteria** — What should the reviewer see or be able to do?

## Review task

| What to review | Task for the reviewer | Question to answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signup confirmation | Register for an event, then find the result | Does the attendee know the signup succeeded and where to go next? |

## Change record

- **Target** — Requirement, flow step, page, layer, or component.
- **Change** — Current behavior and expected behavior.
- **Preserve** — Styles, flows, data, or business rules to keep.
- **Related impact** — Other entry points, states, source files, or instructions to check.
- **Verification** — Starting condition, action, and expected result.
- **Status** — Record what was changed or verified and what remains open.

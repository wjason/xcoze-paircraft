# Quality checks

Choose checks that match the deliverable and the risk. Record what you actually observed. For complex projects, reuse existing acceptance practices.

## Requirements discussion

- Target users, situations, and outcomes are clear. Core requirements map to user tasks.
- Open decisions and provisional assumptions are visible. Ideas outside this round have a place to go.
- The next decision explains how the available options would affect the product.

## Prototype review

- Reviewers know which path to take and which question to answer.
- They can complete the core task. Alternative and failure states are present when relevant to the review.
- Demo data and behavior that has yet to be integrated are identifiable.
- The maintainer can find the editable source and knows how to refresh the review artifact.

## Changes and handover

- Check the revised behavior against the intended result. Verify the preserved parts where the change could affect them.
- When a change spans several places, check that related entry points, source files, and exports agree.
- Hand over each agreed deliverable separately. State what was verified, what still needs verification, and what requires a user decision.
